How it works

Three steps, that's it

Step 1

Point & scan

Open the app and point your camera at your dog's face. No setup, no training required.

Step 2

Instant analysis

Our model reads ears, eyes, and mouth position to score your dog's emotional state in real time.

Step 3

Understand & track

Build a mood history and spot what makes your dog happiest over time.
